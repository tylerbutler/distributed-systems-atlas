# Atlas design critique

**The Atlas has a distinctive, coherent identity. Improve the learning sequence, not the visual world.** The largest opportunity is to bring the next useful action forward and separate the core sandbox from optional collaboration controls.

Scope: the current website source (`src`), with desktop and mobile inspection of the homepage, reference atlas, G-counter lesson, Sets overview, and Dots and causal context sheet.

## Design specificity

**Authored for this product, not a generic documentation template.** Mineral-blue instruments, pale reading fields, sulfur signals, station shapes, notebook illustrations, and ruled records support the subject. Preserve the observatory identity and the analog stories.

The deterministic scan returned **116 advisories: 113 font-size and 3 radius findings**, with no static warnings or errors. Most are differences between specialized diagrams and the documented type scale, not proven usability defects. Rounded data markers are not equivalent to rounded promotional cards.

## What works

- **The homepage gives a clear start.** “Start with counters” is visibly primary; the reference atlas is secondary. It avoids making a new reader choose among every subject.
- **The reading topology supports understanding.** Serif prose, condensed headings, monospaced records, local contents, and the wide-screen terms rail have distinct jobs. After a demo, the page returns cleanly to the reading measure.
- **The sandbox explains outcomes.** One assessment verified Alice's `+3`, all three local values reaching `3`, one checkpoint note, and the literal confirmation “All three read 3 birds.”

## Priority issues

### 1. [P2] Optional collaboration interrupts the core demo

**What:** The G-counter places “Use three devices,” a room-code field, and Create/Join/Copy/Leave controls between the result and the transport/worked-example controls (`src/components/GCounterDemo.astro:98-130`).

**Why it matters:** The learner must process a second interaction model before completing the first. The detour is especially long on mobile.

**Fix:** Put multi-device rooms after the worked example or inside a clearly named disclosure. Keep all three clients and the real kernel behavior unchanged.

**Command:** `/impeccable distill`

### 2. [P2] Lesson openings delay the next useful action

**What:** On G-counter and Sets, the opening illustration and spacing push the lesson map or first comparison below the desktop viewport. On mobile, navigation also occupies roughly 175-195px before the subject (`src/components/StructureLessonLayout.astro:32-44`; `src/pages/structures/sets.astro:16-28`; `src/styles/global.css:371-400`).

**Why it matters:** The pages establish atmosphere well but make learners scroll before seeing how to proceed.

**Fix:** Retain the art; place it beside the introduction on wide screens or reduce its vertical footprint. Bring lesson links before the full illustration on mobile. Compress repeated header spacing without hiding the four navigation links.

**Commands:** `/impeccable layout`, then `/impeccable adapt`

### 3. [P2] Evidence text is harder to read than the main explanation

**What:** The checkpoint log renders at 10.88px (`src/components/GCounterDemo.astro:468`), while the result paragraph has `max-width: none` and reaches about 118 characters per line (`:574`).

**Why it matters:** These are the records readers need to explain convergence, not decorative metadata. Tiny text and long lines make the important evidence harder to inspect.

**Fix:** Raise the checkpoint log to a legible data-text size and cap result copy at the established reading measure. Preserve the tall, newest-first operation lane.

**Command:** `/impeccable typeset`

### 4. [P2] The correctness notice competes with primary signals

**What:** “Not yet reviewed for correctness” is a full-width sulfur block directly below the reference title (`src/components/CorrectnessWarning.astro:1-17`; `src/pages/atlas/index.astro:37-42`; `src/layouts/SheetLayout.astro:62-78`).

**Why it matters:** The same color identifies primary actions and successful demo outcomes. The warning is important and honest, but its treatment makes status and action compete.

**Fix:** Keep the warning explicit and visible. Give editorial review status a distinct treatment, or explicitly distinguish its semantic role from active signals. Do not imply that a visual change makes the content reviewed.

**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### 5. [P2] The reference index initially behaves like a course

**What:** The seven-sheet learning trail precedes territory browsing; “Browse topics by territory” comes after the trail (`src/pages/atlas/index.astro:44-58`).

**Why it matters:** This serves a learner following the sequence, but delays an experienced engineer who arrived to find one topic. Seven links plus the browse action also create eight choices in one block.

**Fix:** Put a direct browse-by-topic action beside the introduction. Retain the causal trail as a clearly separate learning path. Improve this distinction before adding a search system.

**Command:** `/impeccable onboard`

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

**Moderate cognitive load: three of eight checklist items fail.** Single focus, chunking, grouping, hierarchy, and working-memory support are generally sound. One-thing-at-a-time, minimal choices, and progressive disclosure weaken in the sandbox: nine increment buttons, optional room controls, transport settings, and guided exercises are exposed in the same instrument.

The homepage gives reassurance; the notebook story builds curiosity; successful delivery provides a clear learning peak. The review-status warning introduces warranted uncertainty, while the room form interrupts momentum. Returning from the demo to an explanation is a strong finish.

## Persona red flags

- **Jordan, first-time learner:** The counter recommendation helps. The reference trail's difficulty badges and review notice add uncertainty before the reader has chosen a route.
- **Casey, mobile learner:** Visible navigation and large action targets help, but repeated header space and the room form lengthen the route to the worked example.
- **Alex, reference reader:** Breadcrumbs and contents work. The reference index asks Alex to pass a teaching trail before reaching the topic grouping they came for.
