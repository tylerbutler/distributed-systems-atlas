## Planned scope

This lesson will explain how to collect and compare records from a running
distributed system without assuming that the logs show every event.

- Connect local event records through message sends and receives.
- Distinguish an order supported by communication from a chosen display order.
- Explain why missing log records cannot establish concurrency.
- Keep event identities distinct across process restarts.
