## Planned scope

This lesson will separate a loss of communication from a loss of local
progress, then examine what changes when communication resumes.

- Show how local events can continue while messages cannot cross a partition.
- Explain why a partition does not make every event on opposite sides concurrent.
- Follow dependencies on information received before the partition.
- Distinguish restoring a connection from delivering pending messages and
  bringing copies into agreement.
